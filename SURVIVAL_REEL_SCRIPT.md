# "72 HOURS ALONE IN THE JUNGLE" — Complete AI Video Production Script
**Format:** Instagram Reels · 9:16 vertical · 4K photorealistic · ~75 seconds, 12 sequential shots
**Tool:** Muse AI (text-to-video), one prompt per shot, rendered in order, no skips

---

## 0. MANDATORY ANCHORS — paste into EVERY prompt, word for word, never change a single word

**CHARACTER ANCHOR (copy exactly):**
> The same man in every shot: early 30s, short dark-brown messy hair, light stubble, tanned sweaty skin, a small cut on his left forearm, wearing a torn olive-green t-shirt, dark brown cargo pants, muddy black boots, and a frayed red paracord bracelet on his right wrist. Realistic human proportions, natural skin texture with pores and sweat, no face morphing.

**STYLE ANCHOR (copy exactly):**
> Shot on ARRI Alexa, photorealistic, cinematic natural lighting, shallow depth of field, 35mm lens, dense tropical jungle, humid atmosphere with visible moisture in air, film grain, realistic shadows, no CGI look, no cartoon style, no text overlays, no watermarks.

**CONSISTENCY RULES FOR MUSE AI:**
1. Never let the model invent a new outfit, face, or body — the anchors above lock it.
2. End each shot's prompt with: "Final frame: [exact pose/position]" — then start the NEXT prompt with "Opening frame continues from: [same pose/position]." This is how you get zero skipping.
3. Render shots in order 1→12. Do not batch-render; review each clip before generating the next.
4. If a face drifts, re-render that shot immediately with the anchor pasted first.

## 1. SHOT-BY-SHOT SCRIPT (exact sequence, every movement detailed)

### SHOT 1 — The Wake-Up (0:00–0:06) · THE HOOK
- **Action:** Extreme close-up of the man's closed eyes. Eyes snap open, pupils adjust to light. He gasps once, sharply.
- **Camera:** Slow push-in from 30cm away, handheld micro-shake for realism.
- **Movement detail:** His chest rises fast, dirt and a leaf stuck to his right cheek, sweat on forehead.
- **Environment:** Dappled jungle floor light, out-of-focus giant ferns behind.
- **Muse prompt:** "Extreme close-up of a man's closed eyes snapping open in panic, gasping, dirt and a small leaf on his cheek. [CHARACTER ANCHOR] [STYLE ANCHOR] Handheld camera slowly pushing in, humid morning light through jungle canopy. Final frame: man lying on jungle floor, eyes wide open, starting to lift his head."

### SHOT 2 — Sit Up & Realize (0:06–0:12)
- **Action:** Opening frame continues from: lying down, eyes wide. He pushes himself up on his elbows, sits up fast, head whips left then right, breathing hard.
- **Camera:** Circles him slowly 90°, medium shot.
- **Movement detail:** Hands press into wet mud, fingers sink slightly; he wipes his face with his forearm, leaving a mud streak.
- **Muse prompt:** "Opening frame continues from: man lying on jungle floor, eyes wide. He pushes up on his elbows, sits up quickly, head turning left and right scanning the jungle, wiping mud across his face with his forearm. [CHARACTER ANCHOR] [STYLE ANCHOR] Camera slowly arcs around him, medium shot, wet jungle floor, mist. Final frame: man sitting upright, hands on knees, staring forward."

### SHOT 3 — Stand & Scan (0:12–0:18)
- **Action:** He stands up slowly, knees shaking slightly, brushes mud off cargo pants, turns 360° taking in endless jungle.
- **Camera:** Low angle tilt up following him standing, then slow drone-style rise revealing canopy.
- **Muse prompt:** "Opening frame continues from: man sitting, hands on knees. He stands up slowly with shaking knees, brushes mud off his cargo pants, rotates his body in a full circle scanning the endless jungle. [CHARACTER ANCHOR] [STYLE ANCHOR] Low-angle camera tilts up as he stands, then rises above him showing vast jungle canopy. Final frame: man standing still, face tilted up toward the canopy."

### SHOT 4 — Hearing Water (0:18–0:24) · STORY TURN 1
- **Action:** Close-up profile. He freezes. Head tilts. Eyes narrow — he hears something. He starts walking carefully toward sound, pushing aside a large wet leaf.
- **Camera:** Close profile, then follow-cam over his shoulder.
- **Movement detail:** Barely audible → he holds his breath; deliberate slow steps, avoiding a rotten log.
- **Muse prompt:** "Opening frame continues from: man standing, face tilted up. He freezes, tilts his head listening, eyes narrow, then walks carefully forward, pushing a large wet jungle leaf aside with his left hand. [CHARACTER ANCHOR] [STYLE ANCHOR] Close-up profile shifting to over-the-shoulder follow shot, soft distant water sound atmosphere. Final frame: man mid-step, leaf held aside, looking forward intensely."

### SHOT 5 — Finding the Stream (0:24–0:31) · WATER FOUND
- **Action:** He breaks through foliage — reveals a clear rocky stream. His face: disbelief → relief. He stumbles forward, drops to his knees at the water's edge.
- **Camera:** Reveal pan from his back to the stream, then front low angle at water level.
- **Muse prompt:** "Opening frame continues from: man pushing a leaf aside. He steps through dense foliage revealing a clear rocky jungle stream, face shifting from disbelief to relief, stumbling forward and dropping to his knees at the water edge. [CHARACTER ANCHOR] [STYLE ANCHOR] Camera pans past his shoulder to reveal the stream, then cuts angle to water level in front of him. Final frame: man kneeling at stream edge, hands hovering over clear running water."

### SHOT 6 — Drinking (0:31–0:37)
- **Action:** He cups water with both hands, drinks desperately, water spilling down his chin and neck, splashes his face twice, exhales hard.
- **Camera:** Tight close-up on hands and face, slow-motion on the water spilling.
- **Movement detail:** Trembling fingers; he checks the water clarity first (realism beat), then drinks.
- **Muse prompt:** "Opening frame continues from: man kneeling, hands hovering over water. He cups clear stream water with both trembling hands and drinks desperately, water spilling down his chin onto his torn olive t-shirt, then splashes his face twice and exhales. [CHARACTER ANCHOR] [STYLE ANCHOR] Tight close-up, slight slow-motion on spilling water droplets, realistic hand anatomy. Final frame: man kneeling upright, wet face, water dripping from his chin."

### SHOT 7 — Shelter Site Scout (0:37–0:43) · STORY TURN 2
- **Action:** Standing by the stream, he scans the bank, points chin toward a flat dry rise between two large trees. Walks there, presses ground with his boot to test softness.
- **Camera:** Wide shot from across the stream, then tracking shot beside him.
- **Muse prompt:** "Opening frame continues from: man kneeling with wet face by the stream. He stands, scans the jungle bank, fixes his gaze on a flat dry rise between two large trees, walks over and presses the ground with his black boot to test it. [CHARACTER ANCHOR] [STYLE ANCHOR] Wide shot across the stream transitioning to side tracking shot. Final frame: man standing between two large trees, nodding once."

### SHOT 8 — Building the Shelter Frame (0:43–0:50)
- **Action:** He drags a long fallen branch, leans it against the tree fork as the main ridgepole, stacks two shorter branches against it in an A-frame, muscles tensing, sweat dripping.
- **Camera:** Medium shot, slight handheld sway; insert close-up of hands gripping bark.
- **Muse prompt:** "Opening frame continues from: man nodding between two trees. He drags a long fallen branch across the jungle floor, lifts and leans it into a tree fork as a shelter ridgepole, then stacks two shorter branches against it forming an A-frame, arm muscles tensing, sweat dripping. [CHARACTER ANCHOR] [STYLE ANCHOR] Medium handheld shot with a close-up insert of his hands gripping rough bark. Final frame: A-frame branch skeleton standing, man's hands adjusting the top joint."

### SHOT 9 — Roofing with Leaves (0:50–0:57)
- **Action:** He layers giant banana-like leaves over the frame, overlapping them like shingles, top to bottom, pressing each down firmly.
- **Camera:** Overhead-ish angle looking down at the lean-to, then front shot of him tucking the last leaf.
- **Movement detail:** Three full leaf layers; he pats the roof twice to test; a drop of water rolls off — proof it sheds rain.
- **Muse prompt:** "Opening frame continues from: A-frame branch skeleton, hands adjusting the top joint. The man layers giant tropical leaves over the frame overlapping like roof shingles from top to bottom, pressing each layer down firmly, three layers deep, then pats the roof twice and a water droplet rolls off. [CHARACTER ANCHOR] [STYLE ANCHOR] High angle over the lean-to shelter, then front shot of him tucking the final leaf. Final frame: completed leaf-roofed lean-to shelter, man stepping back one pace."

### SHOT 10 — Foraging Food (0:57–1:04) · STORY TURN 3
- **Action:** Dusk light. He moves through undergrowth, parts ferns, finds a cluster of wild bananas on a short tree. He pulls one down, sniffs it, peels it slowly.
- **Camera:** Follow close behind, then close-up on the banana cluster and his hands.
- **Movement detail:** He checks color (green-yellow = safe beat), smells before touching his tongue — realistic survivor caution.
- **Muse prompt:** "Opening frame continues from: man stepping back from the finished shelter. In warm dusk light he parts tall ferns with both hands, discovers a cluster of small wild bananas on a short jungle tree, pulls one down, inspects its color, sniffs it, and peels it slowly. [CHARACTER ANCHOR] [STYLE ANCHOR] Camera follows behind him then closes in on the banana cluster and his hands. Final frame: peeled banana raised halfway to his mouth."

### SHOT 11 — First Bite (1:04–1:10)
- **Action:** He takes a slow first bite, chews, closes his eyes — pure relief. A small exhausted laugh. Eats the rest in two more bites.
- **Camera:** Intimate close-up, warm golden-hour rim light through leaves.
- **Muse prompt:** "Opening frame continues from: peeled banana halfway to his mouth. The man takes a slow bite, chews with his eyes closed in relief, lets out a small exhausted laugh, then finishes the banana in two more bites. [CHARACTER ANCHOR] [STYLE ANCHOR] Intimate close-up, golden hour rim light filtering through jungle leaves, shallow focus on his face. Final frame: man lowering his empty hand, faint smile, looking toward the shelter."

### SHOT 12 — Survived (1:10–1:15) · END CARD
- **Action:** Wide shot: he sits at the shelter entrance beside the stream, knees up, arms resting on them, watching the water as fireflies begin to glow. Slow exhale. He is calm.
- **Camera:** Slow pull-back and rise, revealing shelter + stream + man small in the vast jungle.
- **Muse prompt:** "Opening frame continues from: man lowering his hand with a faint smile. Wide shot of the man sitting at the entrance of his leaf-roofed shelter beside the stream, knees up, arms resting on them, fireflies beginning to glow in the dusk jungle, he exhales slowly, calm. [CHARACTER ANCHOR] [STYLE ANCHOR] Camera slowly pulls back and rises, revealing the shelter, stream, and man small against the vast jungle. Final frame: aerial wide of the glowing jungle at dusk."

## 2. EDIT & POST (assembly rules so nothing feels skipped)
- Order shots 1→12 exactly; each shot's final frame = next shot's opening frame (hard continuity).
- Cuts only on movement (he turns → cut; he steps → cut) so motion bridges the seams.
- Sound design ladder: silence+breathing (1-3) → distant water (4) → stream full (5-6) → birds/insects (7-9) → dusk crickets (10-12).
- On-screen text hooks (add in IG editor, NOT in Muse prompts): 0:00 "He woke up with nothing." · 0:24 "Day 1: water or die." · 0:43 "Shelter before dark." · 0:57 "Food at last." · 1:10 "He survived Day 1."
- Voiceover (optional, record yourself): one flat calm sentence per shot — never describe what the viewer already sees; add only what they can't see ("He hadn't drunk in 19 hours").
- Color: lift shadows slightly, keep green jungle tones natural, no heavy LUT — realism sells this niche.

## 3. ANTI-DRIFT CHECKLIST (run after every render)
- [ ] Same face / stubble / hair?  [ ] Olive torn t-shirt?  [ ] Red paracord bracelet on RIGHT wrist?  [ ] Cut on LEFT forearm?
- [ ] Jungle light consistent with time of day (morning → dusk across the reel)?
- [ ] Final frame of shot N visually matches opening of shot N+1?
- If any box fails → re-render that single shot with anchors pasted first. Never "fix in the next shot."
